## Original note

an acai bowl is a sweet brazilian snack food from Para and Amazons. it is a dish made with the frozen and mashed fruit of the acai palm described as having an "earthy" or creamy taste. its texture is granular before blending and it has a tartness from its acidity, creating a favorable taste. it is served as a smoothie in a bowl.
